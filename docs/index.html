Project 01 / Language Codebreaker Since my teenage years, I’ve been obsessed with linguistics problems—especially those from the International Linguistics Olympiad (IOL)—and that moment when an unfamiliar language starts to reveal its system. I built Language Codebreaker to explore how interactive tools could help young people practise seeing language as code, pattern, and logic. The app currently implements three sample problems from the IOL website, making them more approachable without taking away the deduction that makes them rewarding. Explore project

Interactive linguistics

This browser-based prototype adapts three self-contained linguistics problems with hands-on pattern marking, progressive hints, answer checking, and short explanations—support that builds solving intuition while leaving the puzzle itself intact.
